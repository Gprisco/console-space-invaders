# Console Space Invaders

A terminal-based Space Invaders game where the Player destroys an invading fleet of Aliens with bullets.

## Language

**Player**:
The craft controlled by the person playing the game, positioned at the bottom of the playfield.
_Avoid_: character, user, ship

**Alien**:
A hostile craft belonging to the invading fleet, arranged in a grid across the upper playfield.
_Avoid_: invader, enemy, monster

**Wave**:
One complete grid of Aliens presented at a time; clearing every Alien in it triggers the next wave.
_Avoid_: level, stage

**Playfield**:
The rectangular region of the terminal where the game is rendered and the game takes place.
_Avoid_: screen, window
